Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about studying, financing, and settling in the USA.

What are the basic requirements to study in the USA?

Generally, you need a valid passport, strong academic transcripts, standardized test scores (like TOEFL/IELTS for English proficiency, and GRE/GMAT for graduates), letters of recommendation (LORs), and a compelling Statement of Purpose (SOP).

How does the F1 Student Visa process work?

After receiving an acceptance letter and an I-20 form from a US university, you must pay the SEVIS fee, complete the DS-160 visa application online, and schedule a visa interview at the nearest US Embassy or Consulate. We provide comprehensive guidance for every step, including mock interviews.

Can I get financial aid or an education loan?

Yes! We have exclusive tie-ups with top financial institutions and banks to provide both collateral and non-collateral education loans. These loans can cover your tuition fees, accommodation, and living expenses with competitive interest rates.

Can my family relocate with me to the USA?

Absolutely. Your spouse and unmarried children under the age of 21 can accompany you on an F-2 dependent visa while you study. If you later transition to an H-1B work visa, they can stay with you on an H-4 visa.

What is OPT and how long can I work after graduation?

Optional Practical Training (OPT) allows international students to work in the US for up to 12 months in a field related to their study. If you graduate with a STEM degree, you are eligible for an additional 24-month OPT extension, giving you up to 3 years of work authorization.

Do you help with accommodation and pre-departure?

Yes, we partner with top student housing providers to secure safe and comfortable living spaces near your campus. We also conduct comprehensive pre-departure briefings covering US culture, academic expectations, packing lists, and immigration procedures.
