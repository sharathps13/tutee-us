      Visa Information

      The F-1 route, step by step.

      A clear overview of how the student visa process works, based on official U.S. government guidance. Your counsellor walks you through each stage for your own case.

      1. 1

        Get accepted by a SEVP-approved school

        The school enrols you in SEVIS and issues your Form I-20.

      2. 2

        Pay the SEVIS I-901 fee

        Required for F-1 students before the visa interview. Spouses and children do not pay it.

      3. 3

        Complete the online DS-160

        Upload your photo while completing the form and print the confirmation page for your interview.

      4. 4

        Schedule your interview

        Book at the U.S. Embassy or Consulate — generally in your country of residence — and pay the visa application fee if required.

      5. 5

        Attend the interview

        Bring your passport (valid at least six months beyond your stay, unless exempt), DS-160 confirmation, fee receipt, photo and Form I-20. Officers may also ask for academic records, proof of how you will pay for your studies and evidence of your plans after graduation.

      365 daysNew-student F-1 visas can be issued up to a year before your course start date.
      30 daysYou can enter the U.S. on a new student visa no more than 30 days before your start date.
      InterviewInterviews are generally required, and officers may ask any applicant to attend one.

      1. On-campus jobs

        F-1 students may work on campus up to 20 hours a week while school is in session, and full-time during official breaks.

      2. Curricular Practical Training (CPT)

        Work that is an integral part of your programme, authorised by your school's Designated School Official.

      3. Earn while you learn

        Our team helps you find authorised part-time roles after you arrive — see Earn While You Learn.

      1. 1

        Optional Practical Training (OPT)

        Work in the U.S. for up to 12 months in a field related to your study.

      2. 2

        STEM OPT extension

        Graduates with an eligible STEM degree may apply for an additional 24 months — up to 3 years of work authorisation in total.

      3. 3

        H-1B work visa

        An employer-sponsored visa for long-term work, subject to selection and eligibility.

      4. 4

        Permanent residency

        Employment-Based (EB) Green Card sponsorship is the route many professionals take to settle.

      1. F-2 dependants

        Your spouse and unmarried children under 21 can accompany you while you study. Children can attend K-12 schools.

      2. H-4 dependants

        For dependants of H-1B workers. Spouses may be eligible for work authorisation (EAD).

      3. Our support

        We assist with dependent visa applications, documentation and interview preparation.

      Good to know. Visa decisions are made solely by U.S. consular officers, and rules, fees and processing times change often. Tutee Connect prepares you thoroughly but cannot guarantee any visa or immigration outcome. Always confirm current requirements with official sources:
